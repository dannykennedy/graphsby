_Author, Dreamworker, Founder of DreamTracking, and Certified Life Coach_

Bambi Corso-Steinmeyer is an author, dreamworker, certified life coach, and the creator of DreamTracking—a unique approach to working with dreams as living, evolving sources of insight, guidance, and personal transformation.

Her relationship with dreams began when she was fifteen years old. Over more than four decades, she has recorded and explored more than 8,000 of her own dreams, developing an extraordinary understanding of the patterns, images, emotions, and inner guidance that unfold across a lifetime. Her work invites people to move beyond interpreting a dream as an isolated event and instead follow the tracks that connect their dreams with their waking lives.

Bambi is the author of _DreamTracking: Track Your Dreams and Transform Your Life_, which teaches readers how to engage their curiosity, explore their emotions, recognize meaningful patterns, and develop a deeper relationship with their dreams. She believes that dreams are not simply puzzles to be solved, but conversations that can help us live with greater awareness, authenticity, meaning, and purpose.

A longtime member of the International Association for the Study of Dreams, Bambi presented her work on dreams and grief at the 2026 IASD Conference. She previously served as a review editor for _Dream Network Journal_ for nine years and is a contributing author to _101 Great Ways to Enhance Your Career_. Her professional training includes certification as a life coach through the Quantum Success Coaching Academy, as well as specialized study in dreamwork.

Bambi’s work is deeply influenced by her love of nature, animals, imagery, and the mysterious ways the inner and outer worlds reflect one another. Through her writing, coaching, workshops, and presentations, she helps others recognize the wisdom already unfolding within their dreams—and follow those tracks into a more conscious and meaningful life.

Bambi lives in Southern California.
